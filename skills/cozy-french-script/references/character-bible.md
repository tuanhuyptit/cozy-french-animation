# Character bible — Léa & Mathieu

Persistent facts that regular viewers recognise. Rules:
- Never contradict a fact below. If a script needs a new family member, job detail or object, add it here after the episode.
- Each episode brings back 1–2 old facts in passing (a callback for loyal viewers) and may create 1 new one.
- Facts are small and concrete. No big life events (marriage, illness, moving city) without the owner's approval.
- After writing an episode, list the new facts at the end of the vocabulary file under "Character bible — à ajouter".

## Léa
- Feminine agreements. Organised, precise, curious; often leads explanations, but has funny weak points she laughs at herself.
- Works in communication (ep. "se présenter").
- Loves plants: she waters them and checks whether a new leaf has grown (ep. 02).
- Her mother: the birthday photo album that turned into "un musée" with archives, while rereading old 2019 restaurant messages (ep. 02).
- Her desk is never as clean as when she has a complicated form to fill in (ep. 02).
- Her grandmother: Léa calls her every Sunday around 18 h, her one resolution kept until 2027 (ep. "changer sa vie avant 2027").
- The resolutions notebook of January 2026: 9 resolutions, then the same list copied at la rentrée; the only line written in March was « Acheter un nouveau carnet » (ep. "avant 2027").
- Likes reading in quiet places and looking for a new nice spot (ep. "se présenter").

## Mathieu
- Masculine agreements. Funny, self-mocking, easily distracted, round glasses, curly hair. Often the "bad example" who improves by the end.
- Food lover: cheese above all (comté), the fridge, the "voyage" from the sofa to bed via the cheese (ep. 03).
- His slippers (chaussons): not beautiful, "ils connaissent tous mes secrets" (ep. 03).
- Gets distracted by tiny things: watched a fly trying to leave through a closed window (ep. 02); cleaning his computer turned into finding a class photo and searching for his old sports teacher (ep. 02).
- Walks ten minutes in his neighbourhood after work to décompresser (ep. 03).
- His sister: a good cook; he calls her for recipes and to check facts; she likes old adventure films (ep. 04, "se présenter").
- His upstairs neighbour (voisine du dessus): bakes her own sourdough bread, started in September with a single video (ep. "avant 2027").
- His other neighbour moves furniture in the evening (ep. 03).
- Monsieur Bulle: his sourdough starter, living in the fridge, fed every three days; first loaf was flat "comme une crêpe un peu triste" but great with comté; now bakes on Saturday afternoons (ep. "avant 2027").
- Tante Monique: asks about his projects at Christmas dinner, during the cheese course (ep. "avant 2027").
- His grandfather used to say « Il ne faut pas courir deux lièvres à la fois » and mostly "hunted naps" (ep. "avant 2027").
- Works on projects with associations (ep. "se présenter").

## The two of them
- They say "tu" to each other, have known each other for years, and know each other's habits ("Je connais cette phrase par cœur").
- Running jokes available for callbacks: Mathieu's cheese and slippers, Monsieur Bulle, Léa's notebook, Léa's plants.
