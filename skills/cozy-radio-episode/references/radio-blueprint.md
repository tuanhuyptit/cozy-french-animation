# Cozy French Radio — episode blueprint

Léa alone, cozy radio show, ~20 minutes (2 600–2 800 French words at ~135 words/min, pauses included). Level B1 by default.

## 1. Segments and word budgets

| # | Header in script | Words | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `# Ouverture` | 120–160 | Fixed greeting "Bonsoir, ici Léa, et vous écoutez Cozy French Radio." Mood of the evening (weather, tea, the city), the topic in one sentence, the promise "Ce soir, on va parler de…", a light like/subscribe joke. |
| 2 | `# Le thème` | 1 400–1 600 | The heart. 3–4 beats: Léa's personal story (precise, French daily life, a little funny) → why it happens (simple, no fake science) → what helps (2–3 concrete ideas, each with a counterpoint) → link to learning French. One named image called back at the end. 6–8 vocab items used naturally, 3–4 glossed in one line. A mini-quiz to the listener with the answer a few lines later. |
| 3 | `# Le mot du jour` | 250–350 | "Le mot du jour, c'est…" ONE word/expression: meaning in simple French, why it is useful, 2 NEW examples, "Répétez avec moi :" + one sentence said slowly, a tiny tip (pronunciation, a trap, a false friend). |
| 4 | `# Le courrier` | 500–650 | "Passons au courrier." 1–2 letters: Léa reads a short letter (first name + country), answers kindly with one concrete idea and a link to the topic. |
| 5 | `# La question de la semaine` | 180–250 | One open question; a sentence frame for the comments ("Moi, la dernière fois que j'ai osé…, c'était…"); Léa answers it herself first in two sentences. |
| 6 | `# Clôture` | 100–150 | Recap in 2 sentences, thanks, gentle invitation to The Cozy French (max one mention of Mathieu in the whole episode), "À la semaine prochaine…". |

## 2. Style reminders
- Speak to "vous": "Installez-vous", "Vous savez quoi ?", "Je vous avoue que…", "Imaginez…".
- Rhythm: mix 3–6 word lines with 10–18 word lines. "…" for a soft pause. One idea per line.
- Honesty and humour: Léa laughs at her own weak points (her notebook of resolutions, her plants, her too-clean desk before a hard form).
- After each piece of advice, a counterpoint: "Bon, attention, je ne dis pas que…".
- Callbacks: 1 old fact from character-bible.md, 1 named image of this episode repeated at the end.
- Never "MATHIEU:" lines. Mathieu can be mentioned, never heard.

## 3. Animation scenes (tell the owner which to use)
Host scenes (one per episode for Le thème, don't repeat the last two episodes):
- `studio` — night radio booth, ON AIR, rain → evening topics.
- `grenier` — attic at golden hour, lofi, cat → calm, reflective topics.
- `matin` — Paris balcony, croissant, birds → morning, habits, motivation.
- `train` — train window, landscape passing → change, travel, new start.
- `cheminee` — winter fireplace, snow → patience, comfort, winter.

Interlude scenes:
- `journal` — newspaper front page → opening / key sentence of Le thème.
- `carnet`, `tsf` (vintage radio dial), `metro` (métro line map) → segment changes ("Prochaine station : le courrier").
- `polaroid` → Le mot du jour.
- `lettre` → Le courrier.
- La question de la semaine → back to the host scene or `grenier`.
